97 sophgo/tpu-mlir Edge AI ◆◆◆ High
Problem solved
Compile and quantize trained neural networks into deployable binaries for Sophgo's TPU accelerators, without hand-writing kernels.
How it works
An MLIR-based compiler: imports ONNX/Caffe/TFLite graphs, lowers them through a TPU dialect, applies INT8/BF16 quantization and emits a bmodel for the runtime.
Chinese specificity
Tied to Sophgo's domestic BM1684X / CV18xx silicon — a home-grown alternative to the CUDA/TensorRT stack under export constraints.
Western equivalent ← the bridge a directory never gives you
Apache TVM + a vendor toolchain (à la TensorRT / OpenVINO), but targeting Sophgo TPUs instead of NVIDIA/Intel.

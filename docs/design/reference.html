The calm analytical instrument.

Ink on technical paper, a discreet grid, one cool signal accent, and monospace for everything quantitative. Dense but legible; authoritative without drama. This page is the system — not a product screen. Validate it before we skin the landing.

05 · The fiche in the new skin — decision layer first

97 sophgo/tpu-mlir Edge AI ◆◆◆ High
Problem solved
Compile and quantize trained neural networks into deployable binaries for Sophgo's TPU accelerators, without hand-writing kernels.
How it works
An MLIR-based compiler: imports ONNX/Caffe/TFLite graphs, lowers them through a TPU dialect, applies INT8/BF16 quantization and emits a bmodel for the runtime.
Chinese specificity
Tied to Sophgo's domestic BM1684X / CV18xx silicon — a home-grown alternative to the CUDA/TensorRT stack under export constraints.
Western equivalent ← the bridge a directory never gives you
Apache TVM + a vendor toolchain (à la TensorRT / OpenVINO), but targeting Sophgo TPUs instead of NVIDIA/Intel.

06 · Motion & guardrails

Motion is functional only: 120–160 ms ease on hover/focus, crisp focus rings, quiet detail swaps. No pulse, no glow, no bounce, no motion that implies real-time. Dated snapshots stay labeled “as of {run}”.

Do

  • Ink on paper, hairline rules, a visible-but-faint grid.
  • Mono for all data, scores, ids, dates, labels.
  • One cool signal accent, used sparingly.
  • Muted, equal-weight domain hues; confidence as marks.
  • Small radii, flat surfaces, calm density.

Don't

  • Terminal cosplay / hacker look / neon-on-black.
  • Alarm-red, saturated gradients, glow.
  • Demonstrative “techno” grids or futuristic-AI UI.
  • Floating drop-shadows and pill-everything.
  • Any “live” cue on static, dated data.

Dark calm is a later paired theme derived from these same tokens — not the centre of gravity of the vitrine.

## alibaba/id2_client_sdk
**Type:** Library
**Domain:** Edge AI
**Relevance score:** 67/100
**Problem solved:** Provide an unforgeable and tamper-proof digital identity for IoT devices, ensuring authentication and integrity in connected ecosystems.
**How it works:** The ID² client SDK implements a hardware abstraction layer (HAL) for cryptographic integration and supports four types of security carriers: Demo (demonstration), SE (external security chip), PUF (physically unclonable function), and MDU (security module). The modular architecture separates encryption/decryption interfaces, protocol specifications, and dependent modules to enable adaptation to different hardware platforms. Cross-compilation is configured via make.rules and make.settings files to support multiple toolchains and target environments.
**Chinese specificity:** ID² is a trust infrastructure developed by Alibaba for the fragmented Chinese IoT ecosystem, integrating security standards adapted to the cost and power consumption constraints of connected devices. The project aligns with Chinese technological sovereignty initiatives regarding digital identity for the Internet of Things.
**Western equivalent:** No known direct equivalent - Western solutions (TPM, Microsoft DICE, ARM TrustZone) do not offer comparable unified identity infrastructure
**Maturity:** Experimental (updated 2024-10)
**Language:** Bilingual CN-EN

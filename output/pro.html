02FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. The email brief and the dashboard are free, no sign-up required. The source is Apache 2.0 on GitHub. Pro is an optional paid layer for alerts, exports and the API — planned, not built yet.

Why such a low price for Pro?

€19/month is the price of a lunch — affordable on a personal card without approval. Enterprise is for structured teams with a dedicated tech-intelligence budget.

Gitee only, or GitHub too?

Both. Gitee is the priority source; a GitHub connector additionally captures Chinese vendors that publish only on GitHub — Bouffalo, Sophgo, Unitree, Kendryte and Allwinner are already covered.

Data stored? GDPR?

Free newsletter: just your email, processed by Buttondown (a US-based newsletter service) solely to send the brief. No third-party analytics, no cookies on this site. Pro (when it exists): email + watchlists. Unsubscribe and deletion in one click. Details on the legal & privacy page.
